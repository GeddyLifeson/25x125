25x125

Development feasibility for Chicago lots, for small builders who take a project from purchase through construction.

Open the app

Four questions for any Chicago parcel

  1. What can be built. Zoning district, lot size, height, floor area and parking, read against the Chicago Zoning Ordinance.
  2. What it costs. A dollar estimate with a dollar range, priced by neighborhood from city building permits.
  3. Which incentives apply. Shown at full value, with a switch to turn off each one that doesn't fit your deal.
  4. Whether it makes money. Sale and hold scenarios built from recent sales of new 2-6 unit buildings and condos nearby.

Every number shows where it came from and the date it was pulled. Every default can be changed per deal. The app shows the math; it doesn't tell you what to build.

Data

Chicago Data Portal, Cook County Assessor and data catalog, U.S. Census Bureau and FHFA. Public data, copied into the 25x125 database each night.
