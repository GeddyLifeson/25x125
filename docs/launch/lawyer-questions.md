# Questions for the lawyer (week 10)

Bring the draft terms and privacy policy (`terms-of-use-draft.md`, `privacy-policy-draft.md`).

1. Does showing estimated sale values and maximum land prices to paying users require an Illinois appraisal license (Real Estate Appraiser Licensing Act of 2002) or a broker license? The product labels them as estimates, not appraisals.
2. Is the liability cap (fees paid in the last 12 months) enforceable for business users in Illinois? Should we require that users be businesses or professionals?
3. Is the disclaimer enough to cover zoning results that turn out wrong (for example, a parcel the tool says allows 3 units that the city says allows 2)?
4. Public data reuse: Chicago and Cook County portal terms, Census, HUD, FHFA, BLS. Any attribution or license language we must show?
5. Trademark: "25x125" describes the standard Chicago lot size. Is it registrable for software, or should we file a stylized mark or a different name? Run a clearance search.
6. Arbitration and venue clause: Cook County, Illinois?
7. Privacy: we store email, company name, billing through Stripe, and the deals users enter. Do we need anything beyond the draft for Illinois (BIPA does not apply; we collect no biometrics)?
8. What changes when MLS data comes in later (MRED license terms flow down to users)?
9. Investor and wholesale features are later phases. Anything in V1 terms we should reserve now?
