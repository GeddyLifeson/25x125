# Two-minute walkthrough (script)

Marlon records this in week 16. Screen recording, his voice. About 300 words.

1. **(0:00) The question.** "You find a lot. Can you build on it, what does it cost, and does it make money? This answers that for any parcel in Chicago."
2. **(0:15) Search.** Type an address. "This is a 25 by 125 lot in Humboldt Park, zoned RT-4."
3. **(0:30) What can be built.** Point at the unit count. "Three units by right. Four with an administrative adjustment. The rule and the code section are here."
4. **(0:50) What it costs.** Open the cost panel. "$1,375,000, give or take: $275,000 under to $413,000 over. Every line is editable. Put in your own cost per square foot and it updates."
5. **(1:10) What it sells for.** Click the sale number. "This rests on 14 sales within three-quarters of a mile in the last four years. Here they are."
6. **(1:25) Incentives.** Toggle one off. "These show at full value. Switch off what doesn't apply to you."
7. **(1:40) The answer.** "Profit, return on cost, and the most you could pay for the land and still hit your target."
8. **(1:50) Take it with you.** Click PDF. "One page for your lender or partner. CSV if you want the numbers in your own model."
9. **(2:00) Close.** "Estimates, not advice. Every number shows its source. Try your first parcel free."
