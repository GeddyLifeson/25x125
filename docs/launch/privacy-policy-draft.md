# Privacy policy (first draft for lawyer review)

Draft 2026-10-29. Not reviewed.

## What we collect
- **Account:** name, email, company name.
- **Billing:** handled by Stripe. We see the plan, the last four digits of the card and the billing address; we never store card numbers.
- **What you enter:** deals, parcels you save, cost and price overrides, notes, files.
- **Usage:** pages viewed, searches, reports downloaded, errors, and device and browser type, to fix problems and plan features.

## What we don't collect
No biometrics, no precise location from your device, no data from your contacts, no MLS data in this version.

## How we use it
To run your account, produce reports, bill you, answer support requests, keep the Service secure, and improve estimates using counts and patterns that identify no customer or deal.

## Who we share it with
Service providers who run parts of the Service for us (hosting, database, email delivery, error tracking, Stripe), under contracts that limit their use to that work. Authorities when the law requires it. A buyer of the business, under these same terms. We do not sell personal information.

## How long we keep it
While your account is open, and up to 90 days after you close it, except billing records we must keep for tax law.

## Your choices
Export your deals as CSV at any time. Ask us to correct or delete your account at [support email]; we answer within 30 days.

## Security
HTTPS everywhere, encrypted backups, access limited to the people who run the Service, and an audit log of exports.

## Changes
We will email you before material changes take effect.
